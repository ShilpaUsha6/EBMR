🎵 About This App

This is an emotion-based music recommender built with Flask. It uses AI and keyword detection to understand your mood and suggest songs that match your emotional state.

Features:

  • 🎯 Emotion Detection - Detects emotions like happy, sad, calm, excited, and more from your text input
  • 🎶 Smart Recommendations - Suggests songs based on your detected emotion
  • 🎨 Beautiful UI - Modern, responsive design with smooth animations
  • 📚 Albums - Browse songs organized by artist
  • 💬 Contact - Get in touch with us

Emotions Supported:

Happy, Sad, Calm, Excited, Angry, Romantic, Stressed, Tired
